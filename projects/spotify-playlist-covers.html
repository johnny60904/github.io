Spotify Playlist Artworks

AI-Assisted Contextual Designs & Digital Post-Processing

A collection of playlist cover designs leveraging advanced prompt syntax architectures for semantic image synthesis, refined through Photopea for color telemetry alignment, structural asset masking, and typographic composition.

Design Flow & Philosophy

  • Contextual Prompt Engineering: Anchoring precise mood guidelines, color palettes, and stylistic eras directly inside generative models to maximize prompt fidelity and output consistency.
  • Layer Masking & Post-Processing: Utilizing Photopea to execute defensive layer masking, asset composition, and localized noise reduction to refine the raw digital synthesis.
  • Typography Integration: Standardizing layout structural constraints to position non-obtrusive, high-end branding and contextual text onto the final album canvas.
