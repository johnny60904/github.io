Spotify Playlist Cover System

AI-Assisted Creative Direction

A curated collection of Spotify playlist covers created through prompt engineering, iterative AI image generation, and Photopea-based post-processing. Each design reflects a distinct visual identity shaped through creative direction rather than fully automated generation.

Creative Workflow

  • Creative Direction & Prompt Engineering: Defining visual identity, mood, composition, and stylistic constraints before AI image generation.
  • Iterative Refinement: Evaluating generated results through multiple iterations to improve composition, lighting, visual identity, and stylistic consistency before final post-processing.
  • Photopea Post-processing: Refining color balance, local contrast, composition, masking, and visual consistency.
