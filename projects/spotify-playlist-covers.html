Spotify Playlist Cover System

AI-Assisted Creative Direction

A collection of Spotify playlist cover designs created through human-guided AI collaboration, iterative prompt refinement, and manual post-processing. Each piece represents a creative workflow where visual direction, evaluation, and refinement remain human-driven throughout the process.

Creative Workflow

  • Creative Direction & Prompt Design: Defines visual identity, mood, composition, and stylistic constraints before AI-assisted generation.
  • Iterative Evaluation & Refinement: Reviews generated results through multiple iterations, adjusting prompts and visual direction to improve composition, lighting, consistency, and overall coherence.
  • Manual Post-processing: Uses Photopea for final adjustments including color balance, composition refinement, masking, and visual consistency improvements.

Selected Covers

Each cover represents an iterative design process rather than a single AI-generated result.
